      SoundCloud

      Play your SoundCloud likes and playlists in the SoundCloud tab. Click Connect and sign in with your SoundCloud account in the browser.

      Account
      Not connected
      Download folder
      Use yt-dlp for other songs
      Songs whose artist allows downloads are saved straight from SoundCloud. For any other song you can use yt-dlp, which you install yourself. Saving songs the artist hasn't made downloadable is against SoundCloud's terms, so it's off unless you turn it on.
      Use your own SoundCloud app

      Optional. Registering an app needs an Artist Pro subscription: your apps. Paste its Client ID and Client Secret below. The redirect URI here must be exactly the one saved in your SoundCloud app; if SoundCloud's sign-in page stays blank, they don't match. It has to be http://127.0.0.1: or http://localhost: with a port and a path. Clear both keys and save to go back to the built-in app.

      Soulseek

      Search and download music from Soulseek in the Soulseek tab. yuuricloud does this through slskd, a free Soulseek program it can download and run for you in the background. Use your Soulseek username and password; a name nobody has taken yet becomes a new free account the first time you connect. Soulseek is a file-sharing network: much of what's on it is copyrighted, and what you download or share is your responsibility.
