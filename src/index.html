      Sign in once more in the browser, then scan the code with the iPhone's camera. The phone gets a login of its own; this computer stays signed in.

      Anyone with this code can use your SoundCloud account, so don't share it.

      Downloads

      Download folder
      Use yt-dlp for other songs
      Songs whose artist allows downloads are saved straight from SoundCloud. Any other song needs yt-dlp. Saving songs the artist hasn't made downloadable is against SoundCloud's terms, so it's off unless you turn it on.

      Advanced

      Use your own SoundCloud app

      Optional. Registering an app needs an Artist Pro subscription: your apps. Paste its Client ID and Client Secret below. The redirect URI here must be exactly the one saved in your SoundCloud app; if SoundCloud's sign-in page stays blank, they don't match. It has to be http://127.0.0.1: or http://localhost: with a port and a path. Clear both keys and save to go back to the built-in app.

      Search and download music from Soulseek in the Soulseek tab. Songs you download go into yuuricloud's folder and show up in your library. iOS pauses apps in the background, so downloads only keep going while yuuricloud is open or playing music.

      Search and download music from Soulseek in the Soulseek tab. yuuricloud does this through slskd, a free Soulseek program it can download and run for you in the background.

      Program and network

      slskd program

      If downloads stay queued for a long time, other users may not be able to reach you. Forward the listening port (TCP) to this computer in your router's settings, and set the same number here so it doesn't change.

      Soulseek is a file-sharing network: much of what's on it is copyrighted, and what you download or share is your responsibility.
